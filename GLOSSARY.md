# Portfolio

Karol Wiśniewski's personal portfolio: how he presents his professional profile to prospective clients and recruiters.

## Language

**Pillar**:
One of the two professional directions the portfolio presents: Frontend (primary) and AI Engineering (secondary, clearly visible).
_Avoid_: Specialization, track, focus area

**Role title**:
The single line naming Karol's profession across the site: "Frontend & AI Engineer".
_Avoid_: Software Engineer, Frontend Developer (as the headline title)

**Certificate**:
A course-completion document issued to Karol, shown as evidence for a Pillar; identified by its number and issue date.
_Avoid_: Badge, diploma, credential

**Competency area**:
A named group of skills confirmed by a Certificate (e.g. Context Engineering, Observability & Evals).
_Avoid_: Module, topic, skill category
